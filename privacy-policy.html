Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 Oct 2026

This policy explains how The Royal Consultants handles information shared through this website.

Information we collect

When you use our consultation form you provide your name, WhatsApp number, service, country, city and travel timeframe. The form sends this to us through WhatsApp; it is not stored on this website. We use cookies from Google (Google Ads and Analytics) to measure visits and advertising performance.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, prepare applications at your request, and improve our website and advertising.

Sharing

We share documents with universities, embassies or authorities only when you ask us to apply on your behalf. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

Ask us to update or delete your information at any time on WhatsApp 0307 9499994. Manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
