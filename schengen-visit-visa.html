Typically: valid passport, application form, travel medical insurance, flight reservation & hotel bookings, bank statements. We give you a personalised checklist.
Can you guarantee a Schengen (Europe) visa?
No consultant can guarantee a visa — decisions are made by the embassy. We make sure your application is complete, consistent and well-presented.
