Planning Abroad? Choosing Between Study, Visit and Immigration Routes

A simple guide to the differences between study visas, visit visas and immigration routes — and how to choose the right one for your goals.

The Royal ConsultantsConsultants since 1997📅 18 Oct 2026⏱ 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Visit visas are for short trips; study visas are for full-time education; immigration routes are for long-term residence.
  • Applying for the wrong route is a common cause of refusals.
  • A free assessment helps you pick the right path.

Many people come to us asking for 'a visa for abroad'. The first step is choosing the right type.

Visit visa

For tourism, family visits or short business trips. You must show you'll return.

Study visa

For full-time study at a recognised institution. Often includes part-time work rights and post-study options.

Immigration routes

For long-term residence based on skills, work or family. Eligibility rules are detailed and points-based in some countries.

✦
Consultant tip

Never use a visit visa to try to study or work — it can affect future applications.
